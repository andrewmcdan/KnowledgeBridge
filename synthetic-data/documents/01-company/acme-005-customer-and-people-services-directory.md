# Customer and People Services Directory

> Synthetic Acme Corporation document. Reference date: September 11, 2026.

## Customer-facing departments
Samira Cole leads Customer Success, a 24-person department responsible for onboarding, support, renewals coordination, and customer communications during incidents. Avery Stone leads the 20-person Sales and Marketing department, which owns opportunity qualification, approved messaging, and commercial proposals. Sales may request a roadmap review but cannot promise an unapproved delivery date.

## Employee services
Nora Ellis leads the 12-person People Operations department. It owns hiring coordination, onboarding checklists, leave administration, and training records. People Operations requests access through BridgeDesk; IT Operations executes provisioning after manager approval. A manager cannot treat a completed orientation meeting as evidence that access has been granted.

## Routing requests
Use ClientDesk for customer support and BridgeDesk for internal service requests. Customer Success routes suspected outages to the incident commander rather than asking a customer to diagnose infrastructure. Requests involving payroll, accommodation, or personal employee information are outside this demonstration knowledge base.

## Corpus coverage
Only a small set of fictional role profiles is provided. This directory establishes department totals, not individual salary bands or a complete employee roster. There is no approved salary schedule or international leave policy in this collection.
