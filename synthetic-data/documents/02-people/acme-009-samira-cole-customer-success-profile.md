# Samira Cole Customer Success Profile

> Synthetic Acme Corporation document. Reference date: September 11, 2026.

## Responsibilities
Samira Cole leads Customer Success and sponsors Project Compass, the internal effort to standardize customer onboarding. She owns the customer communication process, support handoffs, and acceptance of onboarding playbooks. Samira reports to Maya Chen.

## Incident communication
Samira or her designated support lead prepares service updates using facts verified by the incident commander. The support clock begins when ClientDesk records the case. A customer may receive a response before a root cause is known; staff must distinguish confirmed impact from an investigation hypothesis.

## Project decisions
Samira approves Compass playbook content and measures whether a pilot customer can complete its handoff. Engineering approves technical feasibility. Sales approves the commercial proposal under its own process. None of these reviews turns a pilot target into a generally available product commitment.

## Current priorities
Her team is piloting the Compass checklist with fictional customer Pinecrest Services and reviewing support-language clarity after the Atlas notification incident. The team uses customer aliases in this corpus and never records personal contact details. Harbor escalation paths remain the same whether the customer arrived through a subscription or an advisory engagement.
