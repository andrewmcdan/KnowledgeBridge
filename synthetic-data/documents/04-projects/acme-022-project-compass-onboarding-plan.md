# Project Compass Onboarding Plan

> Synthetic Acme Corporation document. Reference date: September 11, 2026.

## Outcome
Compass standardizes customer onboarding across Atlas and Harbor. Samira Cole sponsors and leads the project with implementation support from Customer Success. The approved envelope is $8,000. Pinecrest Services, a fictional customer, is the initial pilot account.

## Handoff definition
The target is an accepted handoff within ten business days after all customer prerequisites are complete. Prerequisites include a signed scope, a named customer administrator, approved test data, and validated access. The clock does not start at an informal sales introduction or an unsigned proposal.

## Deliverables and measures
The team will provide a kickoff checklist, responsibility map, configuration record, and acceptance note. Completion means the customer administrator demonstrates the agreed workflow and Customer Success accepts the support handoff. Count missing prerequisites separately from execution delays to avoid hiding the reason for a missed target.

## Risks
An integration without a documented source schema may require a scope clarification. Sales must not commit to custom connectors under the standard onboarding promise. Lessons from the completed Access Cleanup project inform the account-verification step, but employee onboarding rules do not replace customer onboarding acceptance.
