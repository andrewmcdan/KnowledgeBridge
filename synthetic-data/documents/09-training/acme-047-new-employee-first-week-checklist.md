# New Employee First Week Checklist

> Synthetic Acme Corporation document. Reference date: September 11, 2026.

## Before day one
- The manager identifies the role, required systems, and access owner in BridgeDesk.
- People Operations confirms the orientation schedule and training assignments.
- IT Operations provisions approved standard access through SSO. Production elevation is not part of the default account bundle.

## Days one and two
Complete account verification, MFA enrollment where required, and the safe-data handling briefing. Review how to find controlled policies and identify archived or draft sources. Practice a support request using synthetic content rather than a real employee case.

## Days three and four
Review the team's service ownership, change process, and incident escalation path. Engineering staff walk through the release gates; customer-facing staff practice separating response targets from resolution promises. Finance staff review the annual-cost procurement examples.

## Day-five acceptance
The manager verifies access matches the role, all accounts have an owner, and assigned training is complete. Record gaps as tasks with an owner and due date. Lessons from Access Cleanup require checking actual account purpose rather than trusting an account name. No salary, payroll, personal contact, or medical information belongs in this checklist or the demonstration knowledge base.
