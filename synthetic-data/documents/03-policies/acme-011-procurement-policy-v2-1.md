# Procurement Policy v2.1

> Synthetic Acme Corporation document. Reference date: September 11, 2026.

## Scope and effective date
Effective August 1, 2026, this policy supersedes Procurement Policy v1.4. It applies to software purchases and renewals based on total annual committed cost, including mandatory fees. Splitting a request into smaller orders to avoid review is prohibited. Exactly $5,000 is not over $5,000; exactly $20,000 is not over $20,000.

## Approval procedure
For software over $5,000, the requester creates a ProcureFlow request with business justification, vendor details, annual cost, project, and cost center. Finance validates funding; the manager approves business need; Finance reviews compliance; IT Operations reviews security, licensing, and integration; Procurement reviews vendor terms. Purchases above $20,000 also need CFO or documented delegate approval. Procurement then issues the purchase order.

## Smaller purchases and exceptions
At or below $5,000, manager approval and Finance budget validation are required. New software still needs IT Operations review regardless of price. Existing approved software renewals may reuse an unexpired technical review if scope has not changed. Emergency spending requires a recorded CFO exception before commitment.

## Evidence
Keep approvals in ProcureFlow. A budget meeting, quote, or chat message is not a purchase order. Policy questions go to Ravi Desai; financial exceptions go to Elena Ortiz.
