# Procurement Policy v1.4 Archived

> Synthetic Acme Corporation document. Reference date: September 11, 2026.

## Archived policy notice
This policy is retained for historical analysis only. Procurement Policy v2.1 superseded it on August 1, 2026. It must not be used for new requests on or after that date. Its last editorial update was June 15, 2026.

## Historical rules
Under v1.4, software purchases above $2,500 required manager, Finance, IT Operations, and Procurement review. Purchases above $15,000 additionally required CFO approval. Costs were measured using the total annual commitment, not the monthly invoice. At or below $2,500, manager and Finance approval still applied, and new software needed technical review.

## Records in transition
Requests approved and committed before August 1 preserve their original approval record. An uncommitted draft request must use the policy effective when commitment occurs. Do not retroactively erase a historical approval just because the threshold changed.

## Reading older evidence
July meeting notes may accurately refer to these older amounts. That does not make those notes current policy. When answering a threshold question, establish the purchase date and cite the corresponding controlled policy. If the date is missing and changes the answer, explain the ambiguity instead of silently selecting a version.
