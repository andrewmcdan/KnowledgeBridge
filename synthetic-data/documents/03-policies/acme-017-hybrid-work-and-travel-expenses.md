# Hybrid Work and Travel Expenses

> Synthetic Acme Corporation document. Reference date: September 11, 2026.

## Working arrangements
Acme teams agree on collaboration days with their manager. Remote participation is supported for internal meetings unless the event explicitly requires a physical activity. This policy does not establish a mandatory number of office days and should not be used to infer one.

## Travel approval
Obtain manager approval before booking work travel. Record the purpose, estimated cost, dates, and project. Customer-billable travel also needs the engagement owner's confirmation that the signed statement of work permits reimbursement. Approval of a trip does not approve unrelated software spending.

## Expense claims
Submit claims within 30 days after the trip ends. Meals are capped at $65 per person per travel day, including taxes and tips. Itemized receipts are required for expenses over $25. Finance reviews business purpose and cost-center coding; missing evidence returns the claim for correction.

## Exclusions
No international per-diem schedule, mileage rate, visa policy, or family-travel allowance is established here. Ask People Operations for an approved rule rather than extending the meal cap to unrelated categories. Exceptions require a documented Finance decision before reimbursement. Synthetic examples must not include real receipts or payment details.
