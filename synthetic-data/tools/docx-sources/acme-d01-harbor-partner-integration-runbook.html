Harbor Partner Integration Runbook

Owner: Morgan Reed, IT Operations. Effective date: September 15, 2026.
Applies to every Harbor managed integration that exchanges data with a partner system.

When to use this runbook

Use this runbook when a Harbor partner feed stops delivering records, delivers duplicate records, or reports authentication failures for more than 15 minutes. Customer-facing communication stays with Customer Success; this runbook covers diagnosis and recovery only.

Recovery steps

  1. Confirm the alert in the Harbor monitoring dashboard and note the partner feed ID.
  2. Check the delivery mode for the feed:
    1. For webhook feeds, replay the last failed delivery from the Harbor console.
    2. For batch feeds, rerun the most recent batch window only once.
  3. If authentication failures continue, rotate the partner credential through BridgeDesk. Never paste credentials into a ticket comment.
  4. Record the outcome in the BridgeDesk incident ticket within 30 minutes of recovery.

Known gaps

  • There is no documented procedure for partner feeds hosted outside the United States.
  • Harbor does not yet alert on slow feeds, only on stopped feeds.
    • A latency alert is proposed for the fourth quarter of 2026.
