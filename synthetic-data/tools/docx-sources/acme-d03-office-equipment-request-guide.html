Office Equipment Request Guide

Owner: Nora Ellis, People Operations. Last reviewed: September 18, 2026.

Who can request equipment

Every full-time employee can request standard equipment through BridgeDesk after their first day. Contractors receive equipment only when their statement of work says so.

Standard equipment

ItemAllowance (USD)Replacement cycle
LaptopProvided by ITEvery 4 years
External monitor350Every 5 years
Headset120Every 2 years
Ergonomic chair (home office)400Once per employee

How to request

  1. Open a BridgeDesk request and choose the Equipment category.
  2. Attach your manager's approval.
    • Managers approve requests within the allowance.
    • Requests above the allowance also need Finance approval.
  3. People Operations orders the item within 5 business days.

Returns

Employees who leave Acme return the laptop and monitor within 10 business days.
Headsets and chairs do not need to be returned.

Not covered

This guide does not cover mobile phones, standing desks, or equipment for international employees.
